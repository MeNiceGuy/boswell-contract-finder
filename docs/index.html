How notices are screened

  1. Paperwork. Flags requests for financial statements, staff resumes or government past performance.
  2. References. Screens out anything that requires client references or past performance questionnaires.
  3. No live orals. Passes only when the notice states no oral presentations. Possible, conditional or optional orals, and live meetings, briefings or training in the scope, screen it out.

Also screened out: set-asides the firm doesn't qualify for, deadlines under 7 days, staff required on site, security clearances, required licensed credentials, and notices on the already-handled list.

Every match is preliminary. The scan reads notice text, not attached RFP documents, where orals, references and staffing rules often sit.

Unconfirmed notices are kept out of Matches: their text has no express no-orals guarantee, or their set-aside eligibility couldn't be confirmed. They are not fits until the attached documents are read. Use the Show unconfirmed filter to review them.
